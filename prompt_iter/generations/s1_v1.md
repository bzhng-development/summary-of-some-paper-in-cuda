## 1. Executive Summary

This paper proposes a **compute-optimal test-time scaling strategy** that adaptively allocates inference-time computation—either by searching against process-based verifier (PRM) reward models or by iteratively revising the model’s own answers—based on the estimated difficulty of each prompt. Using PaLM 2‑S\* on the MATH benchmark, this difficulty‑conditioned allocation improves efficiency by **more than 4×** over a best‑of‑N baseline, and in a FLOPs‑matched comparison it enables a smaller model to **outperform a ∼14× larger model** on easy‑to‑medium problems, showing that test‑time compute can sometimes substitute for scaling pretraining.
