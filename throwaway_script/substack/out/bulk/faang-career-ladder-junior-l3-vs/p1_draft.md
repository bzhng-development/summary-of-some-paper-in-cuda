Here are the takeaways from this Ryan Peterman article comparing Junior (L3) and Mid-level (L4) engineers at FAANG-like companies:

**Scope & Impact**

The primary distinction between L3 and L4 engineers lies in the size of the scope they can handle independently. Ryan offers a clear rule of thumb: an L3 "can handle individual tasks (<2 weeks of work) with minimal guidance," while an L4 "can handle medium-to-large features (<2 months of work) with minimal guidance." At both levels, engineers are not expected to originate these projects—senior engineers (L5) typically set the initial direction and delegate downward. Because L4s are driving full features, they are expected to perform project management for them: breaking the project into tasks, setting reasonable timelines, and keeping stakeholders updated. The phrase "with minimal guidance" does not mean they cannot ask for help; it means they unblock themselves and make consistent progress. Ryan notes that "asking great questions is one of the most effective ways to unblock yourself."

**Engineering Craft**

Both levels are expected to ship solid, well-tested code, but L4s must take initiative where L3s act when directed. Specifically, an L4 is expected to pursue three areas without guidance: improving the codebase (leaving code better than they found it, initiating refactoring and code cleanups, giving thoughtful code reviews), contributing to production excellence (participating in oncall and debugging production breakages), and owning the health of what they build (adding test coverage, logging, and dashboards to monitor correctness). L3 engineers may do some of these things, but only when directed to.

**The L3 to L4 Promotion**

For an L3 looking to grow to L4, the path is direct: take on medium-to-large features and deliver them with the expected level of engineering craft. If you do that, Ryan says, "you'll get promoted quickly (~6-12 months)." He references his own rapid trajectory—going from L3 to L6 in three years—and mentions a potential "speedrunner's guide" to the L3-to-L4 promotion as fast as possible.
