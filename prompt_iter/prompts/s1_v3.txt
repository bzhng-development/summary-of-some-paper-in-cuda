Produce **## 1. Executive Summary** only.

State the paper's core contribution and primary significance in 2-4 sentences of dense connected prose. Answer: What problem does this solve, and why does it matter?

Required ingredients:
- Specific numbers, model names, dataset names, benchmark names — all the ones that ground the claims.
- The KEY mechanism(s) the paper studies, named explicitly and using the paper's exact terminology for the named concept (do not paraphrase a named method).
- A parenthetical concrete example after each abstract mechanism, so the reader sees what it operationalizes (e.g., introduce a "search strategy" by adding "(beam search vs. best-of-N)").
- Any boundary condition or caveat that bounds the main claim — restate it precisely in your own prose; do NOT just quote the paper verbatim.

Style:
- Open with a verb that reflects what the paper actually *does* — "studies", "analyzes", "introduces", "proposes" — choose by intent, don't default to "proposes".
- Dense connected prose only. Use em-dashes for parallel constructions when listing mechanisms; do not use (1)/(2) numbered lists inside a sentence.
- Preserve hyphenated multi-word named concepts (e.g., "compute-optimal test-time scaling") in full — never truncate.
- Do NOT produce any other sections.
